# 🔥 IronFront 3 — Community Contribution Policy

🔒 **All Rights Reserved**

IronFront 3's original code, models, textures, and other original assets are protected. Unauthorized redistribution of the main mod is not permitted.

🧩 **Community Contributions Welcome**

Players and developers are welcome to create new weapons, models, textures, sounds, and other original content for IronFront 3.

✅ Community members may submit their creations for possible inclusion in official IronFront 3 updates.

✅ Accepted contributions may be integrated directly into the main IronFront 3 mod.

✅ Contributors will receive appropriate credit for their work.

🚫 Contributors must not submit content they do not have permission to share.

🚫 Contributors must not redistribute the complete IronFront 3 mod without permission.

📜 **Contribution Permission**

Before integrating a contribution, the creator must explicitly grant permission for their work to be used, modified, and distributed as part of IronFront 3. Contributors retain ownership of their original work unless separately agreed.
